# Deadline Tracker

## What it is

The tracker in `site/deadline-tracker.html` helps you remember important dates: warranty claim deadlines, response deadlines in demand letters, court filing deadlines, and hearing dates.

## Local-first and private

The tracker saves data only in your web browser (localStorage). Nothing is sent to a server, and nothing is stored by Asdale Dynamics. If you clear your browser data, your tracker entries will be deleted — export or print your list regularly.

## How to use

1. Open `site/deadline-tracker.html` in your browser
2. Add each deadline: title, date, description, and status
3. Mark items complete when done
4. Print the tracker to keep a paper copy

## Important

The tracker is a reminder aid only, not legal advice. Court deadlines are strict — verify them on your court's website and put them on your personal calendar too.
