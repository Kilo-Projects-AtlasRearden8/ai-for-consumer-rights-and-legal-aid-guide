# Free AI Tools for Beginners

You don't need to pay for AI to write demand letters or organize deadlines. These free options are good places to start.

## Web-based free AI chat tools

- **Google Gemini** — free tier, good at drafting letters and summarizing documents
- **Claude (Anthropic)** — free tier, strong at reading long documents and writing clearly
- **ChatGPT (OpenAI)** — free tier, handy for brainstorming and drafting
- **Perplexity** — free tier, useful for finding agency addresses and phone numbers (always verify)

All of these work in a browser or phone app with a free account.

## Local AI (runs on your own computer)

If you want to keep drafts fully private, try local AI that runs on your own machine:

- **Ollama** — runs open-source models such as Llama 3 on your own computer
- **LM Studio** — easy interface for running local models

Local models are best for drafts containing personal details because nothing is sent over the internet. Still, follow the privacy rules above.

## How to prompt AI for consumer paperwork

Good prompts are specific. Instead of "write me a letter," try something like:

> "I need a demand letter to a retailer requesting a refund under the Magnuson-Moss Warranty Act for a blender that stopped working after 3 months. I have the receipt and photos. Keep it polite, firm, and one page. Use placeholders like [YOUR NAME] and [RETAILER NAME] where I need to fill in my details."

Always review AI output before sending or filing anything. AI can hallucinate laws, deadlines, and case names. Verify everything yourself.

## A friendly reminder

AI is a helper, not a lawyer. Use it to draft and organize, but always double-check the facts, dates, and deadlines yourself before you send anything to a company or a court.
