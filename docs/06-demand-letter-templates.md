# Printable Demand-Letter Templates

Print these templates and fill them in by hand, or copy them into your word processor. Replace every bracketed placeholder like [YOUR NAME] before sending. Never include your Social Security number, account numbers, or financial data.

See `site/demand-letters.html` for printable versions of each letter.

## Template A: Warranty claim letter

Asks a manufacturer or seller to honor a warranty: repair, replacement, or refund.

## Template B: Repair-or-replace demand letter

Demands that a company fix a product properly, replace it, or refund after failed repairs.

## Template C: General complaint letter

Tells a company what went wrong and what you want, with a response deadline.

## Each template includes

- Your name and address block
- Date
- Company name and address
- Subject line (RE: ...)
- Salutation
- Body: facts, request, deadline
- Closing and signature line
- Optional "cc" line for consumer agencies

## How to use

1. Open the printable version in `site/demand-letters.html`
2. Print or save as PDF
3. Fill in your details (or type them in a word processor first)
4. Remove all placeholder brackets before sending
5. Keep a copy and proof of delivery

## Never include

- Social Security number
- Bank account or credit card numbers
- Other financial data
