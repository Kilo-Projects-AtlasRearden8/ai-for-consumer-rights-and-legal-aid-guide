# Complaint Letters

## What it is

A complaint letter tells a company what went wrong and what you want them to do about it. A clear, polite letter often works better than an angry phone call.

## Before you start

- Your account or order number (remove before pasting into AI!)
- Dates of the problem
- Names of people you spoke with, if any
- What you want: refund, repair, replacement, apology, or correction

## Step-by-step

1. **Address the right department.** Customer service, executive offices, or a complaints address on the company's website.
2. **State the facts.** What you bought, when, what went wrong, and what you've done so far.
3. **State your request.** Be specific: "I request a full refund of $___ by [date]."
4. **Attach copies, not originals.** Send copies of receipts and photos; keep originals.
5. **Set a response deadline.** 14–30 days is typical.
6. **Follow up.** If no response, escalate to a consumer-protection agency.

## AI prompts to try

- "Draft a complaint letter to [COMPANY] about [PROBLEM] that happened on [DATE]. I want a refund of $[AMOUNT]. Use placeholders for personal details. Keep it polite, factual, and one page."
- "Review this complaint letter draft and suggest ways to make it clearer and firmer without being rude." (paste your draft with personal details removed)

## Checklist

- [ ] Facts and dates written down
- [ ] Specific request stated (refund, repair, etc.)
- [ ] Deadline for response included
- [ ] Copies of supporting documents attached
- [ ] Letter sent by a trackable method

## When to get help

If the company ignores repeated complaints or the issue involves fraud, contact your state attorney general's consumer division, the FTC (reportfraud.ftc.gov), or a legal aid office.
