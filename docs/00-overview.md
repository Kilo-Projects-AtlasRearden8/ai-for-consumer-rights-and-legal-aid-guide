# How to Use This Guide

## Who this is for

This guide is for beginners who want to use free AI tools to handle everyday consumer problems: a product that broke under warranty, a repair shop that won't fix something, a company ignoring your emails, or preparing for small-claims court.

You don't need to be technical. Every step is written in plain English, like a friend would explain it.

## Education only — not legal advice

This guide provides educational information only. It is not legal advice, and using it does not create an attorney-client relationship. Laws vary by state and situation. If your case involves a lot of money, a contract you don't understand, or a court deadline, talk to a qualified attorney or your local legal aid office before filing anything.

## Privacy safety rules (read first)

1. **Never paste personal identifiers into an AI tool.** No driver's license numbers, passport numbers, Social Security numbers, birth dates paired with your name, or account numbers.
2. **Never paste financial data.** No credit card numbers, bank account numbers, or payment details.
3. **Remove names and addresses from documents before asking AI to help draft them.** Use placeholders like [YOUR NAME] and [STORE ADDRESS].
4. **Assume anything you paste into a free AI tool could be seen by the tool's provider.** Don't share anything you wouldn't want made public.

## How each section works

Each topic section follows the same pattern:

1. **What it is** — plain-English explanation
2. **Before you start** — documents to gather
3. **Step-by-step** — what to do, in order
4. **AI prompts** — copy-and-paste prompts you can use in any free AI chat
5. **Checklist** — so you don't miss a step
6. **When to get help** — signs you should talk to a lawyer or legal aid

## The tools you need

- A free AI chat tool (see the Free AI Tools section)
- A word processor or text editor (Google Docs, LibreOffice, Notepad, TextEdit, etc.)
- A printer (for printable templates and the deadline tracker)
- Your web browser (for the static HTML site in `site/`)

Print the templates and tracker pages and fill them in by hand if that works better for you.
