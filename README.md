# AI for Consumer Rights & Legal Aid Guide

A beginner-friendly manual and static website for using free AI to handle consumer-rights paperwork: warranty claims, repair-or-replace demands, complaint letters, and small-claims court preparation.

Written like a friend would explain it — plain English, step by step, never condescending.

## Education only — not legal advice

This guide provides educational information only. It is not legal advice and does not create an attorney-client relationship. Laws vary by state and situation. If your case involves a lot of money, a contract you don't understand, or a court deadline, talk to a qualified attorney or your local legal aid office before filing anything.

## Privacy safety rules (read first)

- **Never paste personal identifiers into an AI tool.** No driver's license numbers, passport numbers, Social Security numbers, birth dates paired with your name, or account numbers.
- **Never paste financial data.** No credit card numbers, bank account numbers, or payment details.
- **Remove names and addresses from documents before asking AI to help draft them.** Use placeholders like [YOUR NAME] and [STORE ADDRESS].
- **Assume anything you paste into a free AI tool could be seen by the tool's provider.** Don't share anything you wouldn't want made public.

## What's inside

- **`docs/`** — the complete beginner manual in Markdown, section by section
- **`site/`** — a static HTML site; open any page in a browser, no build step or server required
- **Printable demand-letter templates** (`site/demand-letters.html`) — warranty, repair-or-replace, and general complaint letters, ready to print
- **A local-first deadline tracker** (`site/deadline-tracker.html`) — stores dates only in your browser, nothing sent to a server

## How to use it

No installation required. Open `site/index.html` in your web browser and click through the topics. Each topic page walks you through what to gather, what to do step by step, and gives you copy-and-paste AI prompts you can use in any free AI chat tool.

Print the demand-letter templates and fill them in by hand, or type your details into a word processor first. Print the deadline tracker to keep a paper copy of your important dates.
