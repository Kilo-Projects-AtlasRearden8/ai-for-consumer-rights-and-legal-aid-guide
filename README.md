# AI for Consumer Rights & Legal Aid Guide

A beginner-friendly manual and static website for using free AI to handle consumer-rights paperwork: warranty claims, repair-or-replace demands, complaint letters, and small-claims court preparation.

## Education only — not legal advice

This guide provides educational information only. It is not legal advice and does not create an attorney-client relationship. Laws vary by state and situation. For legal matters, consult a qualified attorney or your local legal aid office before filing anything.

## Privacy safety rules (read first)

- Never paste ID numbers (driver's license, passport, Social Security), birth dates paired with your name, or financial account numbers into an AI tool.
- Remove names and addresses from documents before asking AI to draft letters; use placeholders like [YOUR NAME] and [STORE ADDRESS].
- Assume anything you paste into a free AI tool may be seen by the tool's provider.

## What's inside

- `docs/` — the complete beginner manual in Markdown
- `site/` — a static HTML site; open any page in a browser, no build step or server required
- Printable demand-letter templates (`site/demand-letters.html`)
- A local-first deadline tracker (`site/deadline-tracker.html`) that stores data only in your browser
