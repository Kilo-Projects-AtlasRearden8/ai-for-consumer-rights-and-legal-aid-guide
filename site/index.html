AI for Consumer Rights & Legal Aid Guide

Use free AI to handle everyday consumer problems — warranty claims, repair-or-replace demands, complaint letters, and small-claims court preparation. Written in plain English, step by step, like a friend would explain it.

Education only — not legal advice. This guide is educational information, not legal advice. It does not create an attorney-client relationship. Laws vary by state and situation. For legal matters, consult a qualified attorney or your local legal aid office before filing anything.
Privacy safety rules: Never paste ID numbers (driver's license, passport, Social Security), birth dates paired with your name, or financial account numbers into an AI tool. Remove names and addresses from documents before asking AI to draft letters; use placeholders like [YOUR NAME]. Assume anything you paste into a free AI tool may be seen by the tool's provider.

What's inside

  • Warranty claims — get a repair, replacement, or refund under a warranty
  • Repair-or-replace demands — demand a fix, replacement, or refund after failed repairs
  • Complaint letters — write clear, polite letters that get results
  • Small-claims court — prepare to file without a lawyer
  • Printable demand-letter templates — ready-to-print letters with placeholders
  • Deadline tracker — local-first reminder tracker saved in your browser

How to use this guide

  1. Read the matching section for your problem.
  2. Gather the documents listed under "Before you start."
  3. Follow the step-by-step instructions.
  4. Copy an AI prompt into a free AI chat tool and personalize it with your placeholders.
  5. Work through the checklist and send your letter by a trackable method.
