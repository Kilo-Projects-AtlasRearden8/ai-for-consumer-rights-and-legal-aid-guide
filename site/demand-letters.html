Printable Demand-Letter Templates

Print these templates and fill them in by hand, or copy them into your word processor. Replace every bracketed placeholder like [YOUR NAME] before sending. Never include your Social Security number, account numbers, or financial data.

Before sending: remove all placeholder brackets, attach copies (not originals) of supporting documents, send by a trackable method, and keep a copy with proof of delivery.

Template A — Warranty claim letter

Demand for warranty repair, replacement, or refund

[YOUR NAME]
[YOUR ADDRESS]
[CITY, STATE ZIP]

[DATE]

[MANUFACTURER NAME]
[MANUFACTURER ADDRESS]
[CITY, STATE ZIP]

RE: Warranty claim — [PRODUCT], model [MODEL], purchased [PURCHASE DATE]

Dear Sir or Madam,

I purchased a [PRODUCT], model number [MODEL], on [PURCHASE DATE] from [RETAILER NAME]. The product is covered by a warranty that runs through [WARRANTY EXPIRY DATE].

The product stopped working on [DATE PROBLEM STARTED]. [DESCRIBE PROBLEM IN 2–3 SENTENCES]. I have attached a copy of my receipt and photos of the defect.

Under the warranty, I request [REPAIR / REPLACEMENT / REFUND OF $___]. Please respond in writing within 14 days of the date of this letter.

You may reach me at [PHONE NUMBER] or [EMAIL ADDRESS].

Sincerely,

[YOUR NAME]

[SIGNATURE]

cc: [STATE CONSUMER PROTECTION OFFICE, optional]

Template B — Repair-or-replace demand letter

Demand for repair, replacement, or refund after failed repairs

[YOUR NAME]
[YOUR ADDRESS]
[CITY, STATE ZIP]

[DATE]

[REPAIR SHOP / COMPANY NAME]
[COMPANY ADDRESS]
[CITY, STATE ZIP]

RE: Repair-or-replace demand — [PRODUCT], repaired on [REPAIR DATES]

Dear Sir or Madam,

I paid [COMPANY NAME] to repair my [PRODUCT] on [REPAIR DATES]. The product still does not work: [DESCRIBE PROBLEM].

This is my formal demand that you [REPAIR THE PRODUCT PROPERLY / REPLACE IT / REFUND $___] within 14 days of the date of this letter.

I have attached copies of the repair invoices and photos showing the problem persists.

You may reach me at [PHONE NUMBER] or [EMAIL ADDRESS].

Sincerely,

[YOUR NAME]

[SIGNATURE]

cc: [STATE CONSUMER PROTECTION OFFICE, optional]

Template C — General complaint letter

Formal complaint and request for resolution

[YOUR NAME]
[YOUR ADDRESS]
[CITY, STATE ZIP]

[DATE]

[COMPANY NAME]
[COMPANY ADDRESS]
[CITY, STATE ZIP]

RE: Complaint — [ORDER / ACCOUNT NUMBER], problem on [DATE]

Dear Customer Service,

I am writing about [PRODUCT / SERVICE] I purchased on [PURCHASE DATE] (order [ORDER NUMBER]). On [DATE], [DESCRIBE WHAT WENT WRONG].

So far I have [DESCRIBE STEPS TAKEN, e.g., called customer service on DATE]. The issue is not resolved.

I request that you [REFUND $___ / REPAIR / REPLACE / CORRECT] within 14 days of the date of this letter.

You may reach me at [PHONE NUMBER] or [EMAIL ADDRESS].

Sincerely,

[YOUR NAME]

[SIGNATURE]

Never include in any letter

  • Social Security number
  • Bank account or credit card numbers
  • Other financial data
